# `atr42_100.project.json` — data sources register

The concept-mode turboprop twin: an **ATR 42-300**, 48 seats, two Pratt &
Whitney Canada **PW120**, category C (no 23.337 cap). The file keeps its
`atr42_100` name — the "-100" was a prototype designation that never entered
production, the -300 is the PW120-powered production airplane, and the name
keys the digests, the CI fixture matrices and the tests.

**Construction rule (the `baron_58.sources.md` precedent).** Every published
value below is cited to the document it was read in. Everything not published
— the planform chords, the tail areas, the control-surface areas, the mass
breakdown, the aero coefficients — is **estimated**, marked **[E]**, and never
presented as the airplane's certified value. Reconciled end to end at #260
(2026-09-21) from the 2026-09-09 capability review §4.

## Estimated values [E] and their anchors

- **Wing planform**: root chord 101 in constant to the engine butt line
  (BL 161), tapering to 60 in at the tip (taper 0.59). Anchored: the published
  span and area [A] — the trapezoid integrates to 586.6 sq ft against 586 —
  and the quarter-chord sweep of the outer panel [C]. MAC 89.5 in at BL 221,
  x-LEMAC 372.8; the WTENV %-MAC limits (10/15/35 %) run through this MAC.
- **Spars and side of body**: front spar 15 % and rear spar 60 % of the root
  chord (stations 380 and 426); side of body at BL 53, half the outline
  width. The 40 %-chord loads reference axis (station 405.4) lies between the
  spars, which note 64 D-64.3 requires of the wing post.
- **Ailerons**: BL 355–470, 25 % of the local chord, 13.8 sq ft per side,
  15 % of it ahead of the hinge; ±15/20° travel. Statistical.
- **Flaps**: BL 53–340, 30 % chord, 53.6 sq ft per side, 30° full deflection
  (the [D] limit); nacelle frontal area 10 sq ft at BL 161.
- **Elevator trim tab**: 3.0 sq ft, 8-in MAC at BL 60 on a 54-in tail chord,
  20° travel. Statistical.
- **Tail surfaces**: the h-tail and fin polylines, areas and hinge splits are
  the pre-#260 entries (unchanged; statistical). The T-tail sits on the fin
  tip: `h_tail_z` 146.2 = the fin polyline's tip waterline above the wing
  root, which is what the tail-height owner assumes when it is not entered.
- **Mass breakdown**: a statistical regional-turboprop component split summing
  to the published OEW [A] less the two-pilot crew row: wing 3,500 lb (9.5 %
  of MTOW; the panel integration is 1,750 lb per side), fuselage 5,300,
  systems 3,600, furnishings 3,200, gear 1,530, tails 760, engines 2 × 890
  (the ENGLOADS record's own weight), propellers 2 × 310 (the record's own),
  nacelles 2 × 320, avionics 597, air conditioning/pressurization 700,
  unusable fuel 47. The empty CG sits at 25.3 % MAC; the whole database
  (43,308 lb, more than the airplane can lift) at 31.7 %. Discretionary rows:
  48 passengers in two cabins, forward and aft holds (600 and 2,000 lb), and per-side wing
  tanks at BL ±250 (the fuel is integral in the outer wing, note 63 D-63.3
  POINT carriage). Reserve fuel 2 × 350 lb is a MINIMUM row.
- **Weight/CG cases**: the eight FLIGHT cases are `seed_flight_cases`'s
  output on this database (decision D-27 and note 63 D-63.5), each carrying
  the searched loading as an entered record (D-25); the gross-weight limit
  points close with a ballast row of 4.5 % of the case weight (the pre-#260
  fixture needed 8 %). The three GROUND cases are
  `seed_landing_cases`'s at the published MLW.
- **Fuselage beam waterline**: 179.6 in, the body's own section-centre line
  mid-body (the wing reference plane at the MAC), so the beam sits on the
  body centre and the LRA owner adds no note.
- **Fuselage stations**: none entered. The beam takes the fifteen stations
  `derived_fuselage_stations` lumps from the item database (design note 36
  OV-1), so the beam carries exactly what the airplane weighs at every mass
  state.
- **Aero coefficients**: section slope 0.1075/deg, cm −0.035, a lift curve of
  0.30 + 0.08 α (deg) and the polar 0.032 + 0.05 CL². Clean CLmax **1.8**
  (a clean stall of ~101 KEAS at MTOW, against the pre-#260 2.009 the lift
  curve could not reach inside the balance's α window); flaps 2.822, which
  puts 1.3 × Vs0 at 105 kt against the published 103 KIAS reference speed.
  CD0 0.032 (0.025 before) so that the whole-airplane polar carries more
  axial force than the wing strips on every case inside the trusted α
  window (`tests/test_balance.py`'s non-wing-drag rule). The pre-closure
  force residual of the balanced PHAA is 2.1 % of n·W, under the 2.5 %
  ceiling; it scales with the stall angle of attack. None are published data.
- **Gear geometry**: axle stations and waterlines, stroke and tire — the
  pre-#260 entries, statistical; the published track and wheelbase [A] are
  reproduced by them within 12 in.

## Cross-checks the file passes

- No `validation.consistency_warnings` on load: the parametric block agrees
  with the planform (area, AR, taper, LE sweep seeded from it), every
  loading CG is inside the WTENV envelope, the wing-mass tie and the
  fuselage partition close, and no typed copy disagrees with its owner —
  `aspect_ratio_wing`, `wing_lift_slope_per_rad`, `wing_span_in`,
  `stall_cl`, the SELECT wing weight and the aero taper ratio are all blank
  and derive (note 36 OV-1).
- Every FLIGHT case is derivable (`test_cg_cases`) and is exactly the seed
  (D-27 drift guard); `mzfw aft`, `mzfw fwd` and `full fuel aft` are the
  D-63.5 seeds.
- The wing post resolves on entered spars and side of body (`assumed=False`,
  note 64), so the LRA deck builds without the assumed-station note.
- `wing_mass.cases` is empty: the ten SELECT slots derive (note 62 D-62.7's
  ruling for this fixture; note 63 D-63.7 makes an entered table a filter).
